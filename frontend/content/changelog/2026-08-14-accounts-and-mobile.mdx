- **Accounts** — [sign up](/register) with email and password, or sign in with GitHub or Google. An account is still entirely optional: the terminal works the same anonymously, an account just carries a higher rate limit and something to come back to.
- **Mobile terminal** — touch scrolling, a fullscreen mode, and a layout that no longer breaks on a narrow screen.
