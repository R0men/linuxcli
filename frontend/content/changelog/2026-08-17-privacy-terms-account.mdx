Three additions for anyone who registers an account:

- **[Privacy Policy](/privacy)** — exactly what's collected (a hashed IP, not the raw address; account email/username if you register), why, and for how long each piece is kept.
- **[Terms of Service](/terms)** — acceptable use and rate limits, written to be actually read rather than skimmed past.
- **[Account page](/account)** — see your username, log out, or delete your account and its data at any time.
